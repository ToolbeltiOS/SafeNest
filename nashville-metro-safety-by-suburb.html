Why "suburb" is the wrong word for this table

The places above are commonly called suburbs of Nashville, but each is a municipality in its own right. That distinction is what makes the comparison possible: because each has its own reporting agency, each has its own figure. An area inside Nashville's city limits has no separate figure and does not appear here.
