Flood & natural hazard risk

Federal disaster records for Tennessee — the state SafeNest uses for Memphis's flood context — list 19 FEMA flood disaster declarations between 1963 and 2021, most recently "Severe Storm And Flooding". Tennessee has also seen 3 hurricane or tropical-storm declarations, events that frequently drive flooding. These are state-level federal declarations, not a parcel-level flood-zone determination — a specific Memphis address may sit inside or well outside a FEMA flood zone, which SafeNest does not determine; check FEMA's flood maps and flood-insurance requirements for that address.

Schools in Memphis

Public schools in Memphis average 3.6/10 on the SafeNest School Score across 198 rated schools. By level, high schools rate strongest at 5.1/10 on average, while middle schools average 2.5/10. The highest-scoring is East High at 9.3/10.
